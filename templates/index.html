How to use Live Capture
  1. Click Start camera, then center your face inside the dotted outline.
  2. Step 1 — Normal: stay at a comfortable arm's-length distance.
  3. Step 2 — Far: step back a little until your (smaller) face fits the outline again.
  4. Step 3 — Close: step in until your (bigger) face fills the outline.
The dot in the top-right corner turns green the instant your face size matches that step — that's your cue to press Capture. All 3 shots stay front-facing; only the distance changes.

How to use Upload Image
  1. Click the box above (or drag & drop) to add exactly 3 photos — JPG or PNG, one clear frontal face per photo.
  2. Each photo is checked automatically: exactly one face, face detectable, image not blurry, and face large enough in frame.
  3. A rejected photo gets a red outline and a "rejected" tag — the reason appears in the status box on the right once checking finishes.
  4. Tap the ✕ on a photo to remove it and upload a replacement.
  5. Once 3 valid photos are in, fill in the name and role, then click Extract features & save.
